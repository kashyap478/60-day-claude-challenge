_***Difference Between Simple Prompt and Engineered Prompt***_

**Normal Prompt**

Gives only a basic instruction.
Contains little or no context.
Does not specify the role of the AI.
Does not define the output format.
Often produces generic responses.
May require multiple follow-up prompts to get the desired result.

**Engineered Prompt**

Provides clear and detailed instructions.
Includes project context and background information.
Assigns a specific role to the AI (e.g., Technical Writer, Data Scientist).
Defines the desired output format and structure.
Specifies constraints and requirements.
Produces more accurate, professional, and tailored responses.
Reduces the need for multiple revisions.
